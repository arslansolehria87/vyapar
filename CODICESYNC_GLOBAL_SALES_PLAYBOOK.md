## Pillar 3: International Lead Generation Engine (گاہک کیسے تلاش کریں؟)

### Strategy 1: The "15-Second Retail Hook" Video Reels (Meta / TikTok / YouTube Shorts)
Run short, high-energy videos showing a real problem:
- **Hook (0-3s)**: *"Stop wasting PKR 15,000 every month on paper thermal rolls!"*
- **Demo (3-10s)**: Show customer paying, shopkeeper typing phone number, and *DING!* customer receiving a branded WhatsApp receipt on their phone with store logo.
- **CTA (10-15s)**: *"Get CodiceSync POS today. Click below for a 7-day free live demo!"*
- **Targeting**: Business page admins, retail shop owners, grocery store owners (Ages 25–55).

### Strategy 2: Google Maps B2B Scraping & Personalized WhatsApp Outreach
1. Open Google Maps in your target city (e.g., Lahore, Karachi, Dubai, Manchester).
2. Search: `"Boutique"`, `"Supermarket"`, `"Organic Mart"`, `"Pharmacy"`.
3. Extract shop WhatsApp numbers and owner names.
4. Send a warm, professional message:
   > *"Hello [Owner Name], we noticed your store [Store Name] on Google Maps. We help retail stores reduce billing paper costs by 50% with instant WhatsApp digital receipts and barcode inventory. Here is a 60-second video demo of how CodiceSync works: [Link]. Would you like a free 10-minute demo at your counter?"*

### Strategy 3: Ground-Level "Market Storming" (Direct Field Sales)
- In the POS industry, 60% of sales are closed **in-person**.
- Hire 2 sharp college students / sales reps on commission.
- Equip them with a laptop/tablet and a portable thermal printer.
- Visit 20 shops daily in a commercial plaza.
- Give a live 3-minute demo: scan a barcoded biscuit, print a bill, send WhatsApp receipt to the owner's personal phone. **Conversion rate is above 20%!**
